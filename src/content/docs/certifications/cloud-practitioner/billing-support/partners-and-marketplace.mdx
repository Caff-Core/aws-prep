## The idea in plain language

A shopping centre has an owner, but the shops are run by others. Some shops sell products. Others offer
services, such as a tailor who alters what you buy. The centre brings customers and sellers together.

AWS has a similar ecosystem. Other companies build products on AWS, or help customers use it.

## The AWS Partner Network

The **AWS Partner Network** is a global community of organisations that use AWS technologies and programs
to build solutions and services for customers.

### Benefits of being an AWS Partner

The exam guide lists partner training and certification, partner events, and partner volume discounts.
AWS also describes co-selling with AWS and selling in AWS Marketplace.

## AWS Marketplace

**AWS Marketplace** is a curated digital catalog for finding, buying, deploying, and managing third-party
software, data, and services. Sellers can be independent software vendors, resellers, or individuals.

Key points:

- Pricing options include free trials, hourly, monthly, annual, multi-year, and Bring Your Own License.
- Charges appear on your AWS bill, which simplifies **cost management**.
- It supports **governance and entitlement**, meaning control of who can buy and use what.

<ImportantConcept>
  AWS Marketplace sells **third-party** products. AWS Professional Services and the AWS Partner
  Network offer **people** who can help.
</ImportantConcept>

<RealWorldExample title="Real-world example: adding a security tool">
  A Mbombela logistics company needs a third-party firewall. It finds one in AWS Marketplace,
  deploys it, and pays through its AWS bill. For a larger project, it hires a system integrator from
  the AWS Partner Network to design its migration.
</RealWorldExample>

## What the exam expects

<ExamTip>
  **Third-party software on your AWS bill** points to AWS Marketplace. **Sells its own software**
  means an independent software vendor. **Helps build a solution** means a system integrator.
</ExamTip>

## Common misconceptions

- **"Everything in AWS Marketplace is made by AWS."** It is a catalog of third-party products.
- **"AWS Partners are AWS employees."** Partners are separate organisations.
- **"Marketplace products need a separate invoice."** Charges appear on the AWS bill.

## Try it yourself (optional)

You need no AWS account for this, and it costs nothing. Browse

you. Note who sells it and what pricing options it offers.

## Summary

<LessonSummary>

- The AWS Partner Network connects customers with organisations that build on AWS.
- Independent software vendors sell software, and system integrators build solutions.
- AWS Marketplace is a catalog of third-party software, data, and services.
- Marketplace charges appear on the AWS bill.

</LessonSummary>
