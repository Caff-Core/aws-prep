## The idea in plain language

When a car breaks down, your options depend on what you signed up for. Everyone has the owner's manual. A
roadside plan sends someone within the hour. A fleet contract gives you a named person who knows your
vehicles.

AWS Support plans work the same way, from free self-service up to dedicated experts.

<ImportantConcept title="AWS has changed its plan names">
  Developer Support, Business Support, and Enterprise On-Ramp end on 1 January 2027. Older study
  material still lists them. The exam guide uses Basic Support, AWS Business Support+, AWS
  Enterprise Support, and AWS Unified Operations.
</ImportantConcept>

A **Technical Account Manager (TAM)** is a designated AWS contact who provides guidance. TAMs come with
Enterprise Support and Unified Operations.

The **AWS Support Center** is where you open and manage support cases.

## Tools that help you monitor your environment

### AWS Trusted Advisor

**AWS Trusted Advisor** inspects your environment and recommends improvements. Its categories are cost
optimization, performance, security, fault tolerance, service limits, and operational excellence. Basic
Support includes service limit checks and selected security and fault tolerance checks. Full access
needs a higher plan.

### AWS Health

The **AWS Health Dashboard** is available to all customers at no extra cost. It shows service health and
events that affect your account, including planned activities. The **AWS Health API** gives programmatic
access to the same kind of information and is tied to your support plan.

## Official technical resources

- **AWS documentation, whitepapers, and blogs** on official AWS websites.
- **AWS Prescriptive Guidance** provides guides and patterns from AWS experts for cloud adoption and
  modernization.
- **AWS Knowledge Center** answers common questions.
- **AWS re:Post** is the community question-and-answer site, included for all support plans.

## Hands-on help from AWS

- **AWS Professional Services** are AWS consulting teams that help design, build, migrate, and manage
  workloads.
- **AWS solutions architects** give technical guidance on designing solutions.

## Reporting abuse

The **AWS Trust and Safety** team handles abuse of AWS resources, such as spam or attacks that come from
AWS infrastructure. If you suspect abuse, use the "Report abuse" option on the
[Contact AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/contact-us/) page.

<RealWorldExample title="Real-world example: an online bank">
  A Sandton fintech runs a production payments platform, so it needs AWS Business Support+ at least.
  As it grows and its platform becomes business-critical, it moves to AWS Enterprise Support for a
  Technical Account Manager. Its engineers check the AWS Health Dashboard for planned events.
</RealWorldExample>

## What the exam expects

<ExamTip>
  **Technical Account Manager** points to Enterprise Support or Unified Operations. **Best-practice
  checks** points to Trusted Advisor. **Events affecting your account** points to the AWS Health
  Dashboard. **Report abuse** points to the AWS Trust and Safety team.
</ExamTip>

## Common misconceptions

- **"Trusted Advisor and AWS Health do the same job."** One recommends improvements. The other reports
  events.
- **"Basic Support includes technical support cases."** Basic covers account and billing questions and
  service quota increases.
- **"Developer Support is the cheapest paid plan."** It ends on 1 January 2027, so check current plans.
