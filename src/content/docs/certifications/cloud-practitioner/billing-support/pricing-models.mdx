## The idea in plain language

There are many ways to pay for a place to stay. You can book a hotel night by night. You can sign a lease
for a lower monthly rate. You can grab a last-minute deal, knowing it may be cancelled. Or you can rent
a whole house for yourself.

AWS compute works the same way. The right option depends on how steady your need is and how much
interruption you can accept.

At the time of review, AWS states that Savings Plans save up to 72% and Spot Instances up to 90%, compared
with On-Demand prices.

### Savings Plans

Four types exist. **Compute Savings Plans** are the most flexible, applying across instance families,
sizes, Regions, and operating systems, and also to AWS Fargate and AWS Lambda. **EC2 Instance Savings
Plans** commit to one instance family in one Region. There are also Database and SageMaker AI Savings
Plans. AWS recommends Savings Plans over Reserved Instances.

### Spot Instances

Spot Instances use spare EC2 capacity. AWS can reclaim them with a **two-minute warning**, so they suit
batch jobs, data analysis, and other interruptible work. They do not suit a production database that must
keep running.

### Dedicated Hosts and Dedicated Instances

Both give you hardware that is not shared with other customers. **Dedicated Hosts** add visibility and
control over the physical server, and they give fuller support for Bring Your Own License. Dedicated Instances
do not offer that placement control.

### Capacity Reservations

**On-Demand Capacity Reservations** guarantee capacity, but they carry no discount of their own. You can
combine them with Savings Plans or regional Reserved Instances to get one.

<ImportantConcept>
  A discount and a capacity guarantee are different things. Savings Plans give a discount. Capacity
  Reservations give certainty. A zonal Reserved Instance gives both.
</ImportantConcept>

## Reserved Instance flexibility

A Reserved Instance (RI) is a billing discount, not a physical server. You choose:

- **Term:** one year or three years. Three years gives a larger discount.
- **Payment:** All Upfront, Partial Upfront, or No Upfront.
- **Class:** see the table below.
- **Scope:** regional or zonal.

RIs do not renew automatically. When they expire, you pay On-Demand rates.

### Reserved Instances in AWS Organizations

With consolidated billing, an organization's accounts are treated as one for discounts, so one account's
RIs can benefit the others. This sharing can be turned off in the Billing console.

## Data transfer costs

Data moving between places may be charged. The rule of thumb is that data coming in is free, and data
going out or across boundaries usually is not.

## Storage pricing

Storage has several cost components. For Amazon S3, these include the amount stored, requests and data
retrieval, data transfer, and management features. The storage class matters too. Colder classes cost
less to store, but they can add retrieval fees and minimum storage durations.

<CostWarning>
  Unexpected bills often come from data transfer and forgotten resources. Check the pricing page of
  each service, because rates vary by Region.
</CostWarning>

<RealWorldExample title="Real-world example: mixing options">
  A Cape Town streaming company runs its core website all year, so it buys a Compute Savings Plan.
  New features run On-Demand while usage is unknown. Overnight video conversion uses Spot Instances,
  because a job can simply restart if interrupted.
</RealWorldExample>

## What the exam expects

<ExamTip>
  **Steady, long-term** points to Savings Plans or Reserved Instances. **Short or unpredictable**
  points to On-Demand. **Can tolerate interruption** points to Spot. **Server-bound licenses**
  points to Dedicated Hosts. **Guaranteed capacity, no commitment** points to Capacity Reservations.
</ExamTip>

## Common misconceptions

- **"A Reserved Instance is a server."** It is a billing discount.
- **"Spot Instances are cheap On-Demand."** They can be interrupted.
- **"All data transfer is charged."** Inbound data transfer has no charge.

## Summary

<LessonSummary>

- On-Demand has no commitment. Savings Plans and Reserved Instances reward commitment.
- Spot Instances are heavily discounted but can be interrupted.
- Dedicated Hosts and Dedicated Instances provide dedicated hardware.
- Capacity Reservations guarantee capacity, without a discount of their own.
- Inbound data transfer has no charge. Transfer across Availability Zones, Regions, and out to the
  internet is charged.

</LessonSummary>
