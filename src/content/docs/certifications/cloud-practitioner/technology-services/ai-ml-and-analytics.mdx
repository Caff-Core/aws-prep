## The idea in plain language

A company can build its own translator from scratch, or it can hire one. Most companies hire, because
building one is slow and expensive.

**Artificial intelligence (AI)** means software doing tasks that normally need human intelligence.
**Machine learning (ML)** is a way of building AI, where models learn from data. AWS offers ready-made AI
services you can call directly, plus tools for building your own models.

**Analytics** means turning raw data into useful answers.

You do not need to know how these services work inside. For this exam, match each service to its task.

## Building your own models

**Amazon SageMaker AI** is a managed service for building, training, and deploying machine learning
models. If a scenario involves data scientists creating custom models, this is the answer.

<RealWorldExample title="Real-world example: a call center">
  An East London insurer records support calls. Amazon Transcribe turns them into text. Amazon
  Comprehend finds whether customers sound satisfied. The results land in Amazon S3, where analysts
  query them with Amazon Athena and share dashboards with managers.
</RealWorldExample>

## What the exam expects

<ExamTip>
  Learn the verb for each service. **Build models** means SageMaker AI. **Chatbot** means Lex.
  **Analyse images** means Rekognition. **Extract from documents** means Textract. **Query Amazon S3
  with SQL** means Athena. **Real-time streaming** means Kinesis.
</ExamTip>

## Common misconceptions

- **"I need ML skills to use these services."** Ready-made services are called through an API.
- **"Textract and Transcribe do the same thing."** Textract reads documents. Transcribe listens to speech.
- **"Athena needs a database server."** It is serverless and queries data in Amazon S3.

## Summary

<LessonSummary>

- Amazon SageMaker AI builds, trains, and deploys custom models.
- Ready-made services handle speech, language, images, documents, and chatbots.
- Athena queries Amazon S3 with SQL, Kinesis handles streams, and Glue prepares data.
- Amazon Redshift and Amazon Quick support analysis and business insights.

</LessonSummary>
